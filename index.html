Emmanuel Barbe, 48, trader. Based in Thailand for 12 years, father of 3.

In March 2020 I founded CryptoFeel: first a Telegram channel, then a website and a full blockchain academy, a true school of alternative finance.

I then worked as a contract trader for several companies: Futures (2021–2025), iGenius (a subsidiary of US-based Investview) and The Investor. I was on the trading side, never in management.

My original specialty: accumulating Bitcoin by trading altcoins against it, spot on Binance. I then expanded into the stock market (my equity portfolio is public on Moning), then CFDs, gold and indices. My playing field: the whole investment pyramid, from real estate up to crypto.

Today, with First Light, I'm back to basics: every morning, my analysis and every trade published, wins and losses alike.
